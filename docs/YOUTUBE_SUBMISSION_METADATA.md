🌿 WHAT IS WELLSYNC?
In Abu Dhabi, healthy habits collapse not from a lack of will, but when real life hits: extreme summer heat (>45°C), 12-hour rotating hospital night shifts (SEHA/M42), and Ramadan circadian shifts. When routines are disrupted, conventional fitness apps punish users with streak resets to zero.

Psychologists call this the "What-the-Hell Effect" (Marlatt & Gordon): one missed day feels like total failure, and users quit. WellSync was built on a fundamentally different principle: habit durability is not measured by uninterrupted perfection. It is defined by the speed and ease of recovery after disruption.

WellSync replaces punitive resets with an adaptive 50% Minimum Target Ladder (BCT 8.7), preserves cumulative active days, and celebrates every rebound with a dedicated Return Count metric.

🔬 VERIFIED OPEN-SCIENCE EVIDENCE:
We benchmarked our deterministic engine against 71 real participants and 7,443 participant-days from the peer-reviewed LifeSnaps wearable dataset (Sofia Yfantidou et al., Nature Scientific Data 2022, DOI: 10.1038/s41597-022-01764-x):
• Policy 1 (Static 10,000 steps): Labeled 89.4% of days as failure during disruptions.
• Policy 2 (Personal Baseline with Streak Reset): Triggered an average of 17.9 catastrophic zero-resets per user.
• Policy 4 (WellSync Adaptive Ladder): Achieved 0.0 streak resets by design, maintained 31.9% active rung feasibility during disruptions, and achieved an average recovery rebound in just 3.1 days.

🛡️ LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY & ZERO RUNTIME AI:
• 100% Local-First: User telemetry is processed directly in the browser enclave. Zero unauthorized network calls, fully verified in Chrome DevTools.
• UAE PDPL Compliant: Fully aligns with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021 on Personal Data Protection.
• Deterministic & Auditable: Zero black-box LLMs or hallucinations. Every state transition is transparent and grounded in the Michie BCTTv1 behavioral taxonomy.

🇦🇪 BUILT NATIVELY FOR THE UAE:
• Ramadan Fasting Mode: Offline Hijri calendar calculations, 70% daytime scaling, and activity prompt windows post-Iftar (8:30 PM–11:00 PM) and pre-Suhoor.
• Extreme Summer Heat Mode: Triggers at >38°C to swap outdoor routes with air-conditioned mall-walking loops (Abu Dhabi Mall, Yas Mall).
• SEHA Shift-Work Mode: Calibrated for 2-Day, 2-Night, 4-Off rosters with 60% night shift scaling and non-penalized recovery sleep.
• Full Arabic RTL Localization: 1-click bidirectional language toggle with mirrored layouts and typography.
