2. The 4-State Behavioral Recovery State Machine

Replaces punitive Duolingo-style streak resets with an adaptive recovery loop that eliminates the Marlatt & Gordon "What-the-Hell Effect".

4. Resilient Minimum Ladder Architecture (BCT 8.7)

Formal graded task architecture preventing relapse during disruption events:

▲ Stretch Rung (110% = 8,250 Steps)
Peak capacity days, high energy, surplus motivation. Encourages upward expansion without penalty.
◼ Baseline Rung (100% = 7,500 Steps)
Standard daily operational target calibrated to personal baseline. Maintains habitual rhythm.
▼ Minimum Resilient Rung (50% = 3,750 Steps)
BCT 8.7 Graded Tasks: Activated during shift work, illness, or extreme heat. Counts as a full success day.
