## 1. Executive Strategy: Why This Video Wins

Judges evaluate dozens of submissions. Most teams make the same fatal mistakes:
- ❌ Spending 90 seconds explaining common health problems before showing their product.
- ❌ Showing static Figma slides instead of a live, working codebase.
- ❌ Promising "magical AI" that hallucinates or cannot be audited.
- ❌ Forgetting the specific cultural and environmental context of Abu Dhabi.

### The WellSync Video Formula:
1. **The Instant Hook (0:00–0:25)**: Hit the emotional and scientific core immediately—why digital health habits fail in Abu Dhabi and why streak resets are the hidden villain.
2. **Instant Live Proof (0:25–0:50)**: No mockups. 1-click launch into the live, working application with our built-in **"⚡ Judge Demo"** mode.
3. **The Drama of the Disruption (0:50–1:20)**: Show a real human scenario (Tariq, an Abu Dhabi nurse on a 3-day night shift). Watch the deterministic engine gracefully step down to the **50% Minimum Target** instead of wiping out his streak.
4. **The Triumph of Recovery (1:20–1:50)**: Complete the 3-day recovery ladder, celebrate the increment of the **Return Count**, and show that active days are permanently saved.
5. **Open Science & Abu Dhabi Depth (1:50–2:40)**: Walk through the **LifeSnaps 71-participant evidence matrix**, flip into **Arabic RTL mode**, and show **0 network calls** in Chrome DevTools.
6. **The Decisive Closer (2:40–3:00)**: Deliver our clear path to pilot with SEHA/M42, state our student status with pride, and close on our memorable thesis.

## 4. Rehearsal & Recording Checklist

Before hitting record in OBS Studio:
- [ ] Run `npm run dev -- --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5173`.
- [ ] Open Chrome at `http://localhost:5173/`.
- [ ] Press `F11` (or hide bookmarks bar with `Ctrl+Shift+B`).
- [ ] Ensure the browser zoom is exactly `100%`.
- [ ] Test the `⚡ Judge Demo (90s)` button to ensure Tariq's scenario loads instantly.
- [ ] Test the Arabic language toggle to confirm smooth RTL transition.
- [ ] Open DevTools (`Ctrl+Shift+I`), dock it to the bottom, and clear network history to demonstrate zero outbound requests.
- [ ] Record a 15-second audio test in Audacity or OBS to verify microphone volume (peaks between -6 dB and -3 dB, zero background hum).
- [ ] Time your rehearsal with a stopwatch: Aim for **02:55** so you never rush or get cut off.
